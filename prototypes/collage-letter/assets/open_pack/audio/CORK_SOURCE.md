# Recorded cork sounds

Source: [Fantasy Accessory SFX Library](https://opengameart.org/content/fantasy-accessory-sfx-library), Vehicle / Jan Schupke, 2015-08-13.
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/accessory.zip
License: [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The author describes the recordings as organic, unprocessed apart from normalization.

- `cork-pop.wav`: unchanged `sfx/bottle-glass-uncork-02.wav`.
- `cork-close.wav`: unchanged `sfx/bottle-glass-cork-01.wav`.

Both are stereo 44.1 kHz PCM16 field recordings, not synthesized sound. The uncork event plays the full approximately 0.60-second pop. Re-sealing uses its own recording. The softer cork-on-table event reuses a short section of the closing recording; it is not claimed as a separate desk-drop recording.
