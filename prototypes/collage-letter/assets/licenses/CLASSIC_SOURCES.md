# Classic excerpt sources

The text clippings use original public-domain works, never invented passages or modern translations. Traditional characters and punctuation follow the cited transcription. A clipping may be a contiguous fragment of a longer sentence; its full attribution remains in materials.json.

- Ancient Tang poems: chinese-poetry/Tang 300 transcription, MIT. Preserve chinese-poetry-MIT.txt. https://github.com/chinese-poetry/chinese-poetry/blob/master/全唐诗/唐诗三百首.json
- Kenneth Grahame (1859–1932), The Wind in the Willows (1908), original English. Project Gutenberg edition 289: https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/289 . Original text is public domain in the US and countries using life+70. No modern translation or illustrated edition is reused.
- Scott Joplin, Original Rags (1899): existing public-domain score scan, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Original_Rags.pdf . The 17 score clippings are excerpts from the four scanned pages, not 17 different compositions.

Maya's bookshop and resident album labels are fictional in-world provenance. They do not replace the real source/licensing records. Existing drafts retain their original text scraps through the versioned legacy material catalog.
